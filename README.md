# Lumenstru AI

Lumenstru AI is a phase-aware Android study planner. It predicts your cycle phases from your own period logs, estimates your study energy for each day and moves demanding tasks to better days without ever changing a deadline. It also has a calmer Lavender Mode during your period, a Calm Space with audio, breathing and stretches, insights and a doctor summary PDF.

Built as a CS6008 Capstone Project at Laksamana College of Business (2026).

## Download

Get the latest APK from the [Releases](../../releases/latest) page.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded `LumenstruAI.apk` on an Android phone (Android 7.0 or later).
2. If asked, allow installing apps from this source.
3. Open Lumenstru AI and follow the setup steps.

No account is needed. Your data stays on your phone unless you choose to sign in.
